# **COMMUNITY GUIDELINES**

These Guidelines aim to promote a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone on the social media platform. Moderation of content on the platform is grounded in respect for human rights and fundamental freedoms, while recognizing that the laws must be followed in different jurisdictions. Proportional enforcement actions are used that prioritize community safety while preserving space for legitimate expression and open dialogue.

Enforcing baseline rules require careful judgment. That means difficult decisions need to be made about whether certain types of content or expression cross into becoming harassment or other prohibited conduct.

## **Our Principles:**

- Safety First: Content that shows or promotes violence, harm, exploitation, or criminal activity against adults, children, or animals are not allowed. Content should not violate anyone's privacy or share personal information.
- Respect Others: Harassment, bullying, hate speech, or discrimination are not allowed. This includes targeting individuals based on protected characteristics.
- Be Authentic: Content intended to misrepresent, defraud, trick, deceive, or mislead is not allowed.
- Follow the Rules: Content or behavior that violates applicable laws, breaks platform policies, compromises the platform's systems, or evades enforcement actions are not allowed.

## **1\. Safety First**

- **Public Safety**: The platform keeps the community safe by stopping people from using the platform for criminal activities. A social media post should not:
  - Coordinate, promote, recruit or disseminate content for criminal organizations, terrorist groups, or human trafficking networks.
  - Provide instruction for criminal or terrorist activities:
    - Detailed tutorials for committing crimes or terrorist acts.
    - Coordination of harmful acts, including swatting or mob violence.
  - Share violence content for harmful purposes:
    - Content serving criminal or terrorist organizational operations.
    - Videos exploiting victims of abuse or violence.
- **Respectful Discourse** The platform creates a safe space for discussion by prohibiting threats and content meant to scare or intimidate people. A social media post should not:
  - Threaten others with death, serious harm, or express that others should be hurt or killed.
  - Promote, glorify, or incite violence against individuals or groups.
  - Share graphic violent content designed to shock, disturb, or intimidate others.
  - These rules do not prevent one from posting content that involves violence without glorifying real-world harm, including:
    - Professional combat sports, martial arts demonstrations, and regulated extreme sports.
    - Discussion, analysis, or depiction of violence in clearly fictional or artistic contexts such as literature, film, television, video games, and visual arts.
- **Child Safety:** The platform protects kids and teens with strong safeguards against exploitation, predatory behavior, and harmful content, implementing additional measures where legally required. A social media post should not:
  - Create, share, or promote content that harms minors: Content that sexualizes, exploits, or depicts minors or minor-aged characters in sexual or intimate contexts, including synthetic, simulated, illustrated, or animated versions.
  - Engage in inappropriate conduct toward minors:
    - Grooming behavior or soliciting intimate content, personal information, or offline meetings from minors.
    - Coercing minors to create or share intimate content.
  - Share personal information about minors: Identifying information, real-time location data, or personal details that could pose safety risks.
  - Engage in commercial practices targeting minors, such as prohibited advertising that targets underage users, or promoting gambling, controlled substances, tobacco, alcohol, or deceptive financial services to minors.
- **Adult Content:** The platform provides a safe space for consensual adult expression by requiring clear consent and stopping abuse.
  - The platform allows consensual adult sexual content, including fictional depictions, when appropriately labeled and subject to appropriate age restrictions.
  - The platform does not allow sexual content involving non-consensual activity including synthetic, simulated, illustrated, or animated versions.
  - Social media posts should not share, threaten to share, or promote tools to create intimate images or sexual content of any individual without their express consent, including synthetic or digitally altered content.
  - Social media posts should not create, share, or promote sexual content involving realistic risk of death or serious injury.
  - One should not engage in technology-facilitated abuse, harassment, or unwanted advances.
- **Mental Health & Wellbeing:** The platform supports a healthier community by prohibiting content that could harm people's mental or physical health. A social media post should not:
  - Share content, methods, instructions, or promotion of self-harm, suicide, eating disorders, and extreme dieting practices.
  - Share methods, instructions, depictions, or promotion of dangerous stunts or abuse of dangerous or controlled substances.
- **Privacy Protection:** The platform protects people's privacy by prohibiting unauthorized sharing of personal information and private content. A social media post should not
  - Share home addresses, personal contact info, financial or medical data, or identification documents.
  - Share sensitive private messages or communications you weren't authorized to share, unless sharing is in the public's interest.
  - Share real-time location or other information that could pose immediate safety risks.
- **Animal Safety:** The platform protects animals by prohibiting abusive or exploitative content.
  - One should not create, share, or promote sexual content involving real animals or realistically depicted synthetic, simulated, illustrated, or animated versions.
  - One should not share content depicting animal abuse, torture, fighting, staged rescues, or wildlife exploitation.
  - These rules do not prevent one from sharing content about hunting, fishing, and other legal wildlife activities.

## **2\. Respect Others**

- **Anti-Discrimination**: The platform protects people's fundamental rights by prohibiting identity-based harm and hatred.
  - Social media posts should not attack, harass, or incite hatred or discrimination against individuals or groups based on protected characteristics such as age, asylum-seeker/refugee status, caste, disability, disease, ethnicity, gender identity, immigrant status, race, religious affiliation, sex, or sexual orientation.
  - This includes hate speech, slurs, dehumanizing comparisons, supremacist content, conspiracy theories targeting protected groups, and coordinated harassment campaigns.
- **Anti-Harassment**: The platform creates space for everyone to express themselves by prohibiting harassment and toxic behaviors.
  - One should not stalk, persistently target, or create malicious content designed to humiliate or degrade individuals, including through coordinated campaigns.
  - One should not abuse the platform features (lists, labels, community moderation tools), engage in bad-faith mass reporting, use automated harassment systems, or create single-purpose harassment accounts.
  - Accounts that engage in extreme or persistent toxic or disruptive behavior may face restrictions or removal. This includes accounts with a high number or proportion of:
    - Extreme or repeated personal attacks, such as insults or ad hominem attacks
    - Posts or responses made primarily to anger, provoke, or belittle instead of to contribute criticism or a genuine opinion
    - The use of hostile or inflammatory language clearly intended to create or contribute to an unwelcoming environment

## **3\. Be Authentic**

- **Trust & Transparency**: The platform is kept trustworthy by prohibiting deceptive and manipulative practices.
  - One should not send spam or repeatedly post content in ways that disrupt normal conversations or service use.
  - One should not create, share, or promote financial scams, phishing attempts, or fraudulent fundraising.
  - One should not artificially manipulate features or social signals to gain unearned reach or mislead users, including engagement metrics, follower counts, or other measures of community interest.
  - One should not post undisclosed commercial content, sponsored material, or advertising without clearly identifying its commercial nature to other users.
- **Account Authenticity**: The platform keeps its community trustworthy by prohibiting impersonation and deceptive account practices.
  - One should not impersonate others or official groups in ways that could mislead users, or create fake accounts to deceive others about who you are.
  - One should not engage in deceptive account practices, including identity churning (changing one's account identity to keep followers), handle-squatting, coordinated deception, or other deceptive behaviors.
  - One should not abuse platform Trust & Safety systems by:
    - Falsifying documents or misrepresenting one's verification status.
    - Selling, transferring, or sharing accounts.
    - Attempting to bypass age requirements and age assurance processes (where applicable in certain regions) by misrepresenting one's age or helping others do so.
  - These rules do not prevent one from using alternative identities without misleading other users, including:
    - Clearly labeled parody, satire, or fan accounts that identify their nature in both display name and bio.
    - Clearly labeled fictional character accounts.
    - Historical, educational, or artistic content that uses public figures or institutions with proper context.
- **Information integrity:** The platform allows reliable information sharing while prohibiting content that could undermine civic processes or is likely to cause immediate real-world harm.
  - One should not share false or misleading information that could cause immediate real-world harm, including coordinating inauthentic campaigns.
  - One should not coordinate to suppress voting, impersonate electoral officials or campaigns, interfere with democratic processes, or share false information about voting procedures, locations, times, eligibility, or results.

## **4\. Follow the Rules**

- **Regulated Goods & Services**: The platform keeps its community safe by prohibiting commerce in restricted items. It should not be used to unlawfully sell, advertise, provide services for, or facilitate commercial transactions for:
  - Controlled substances, illegal drugs, prescription medications, or drug paraphernalia.
  - Weapons, firearms, ammunition, or other dangerous materials.
  - Stolen goods, counterfeit items, forged documents, criminal property, live animals, or other restricted goods.
- **Intellectual property & Copyright:** The platform protects creators' rights by prohibiting content theft and copyright violations. One should not infringe copyrights, steal content, remove attribution or watermarks, violate trademarks or patents, impersonate brands or businesses, or share unauthorized streams or downloads.
- **Site Security**: The platform is kept running smoothly by stopping attacks on its systems and interference. One should not attempt to compromise, exploit, bypass, abuse, or disrupt the platform's systems, security features, APIs, rate limits, or infrastructure.
- **Repeated Violations & Ban Evasion:** The platform keeps its rules fair by prohibiting people from getting around bans and other enforcement actions. One should not create new accounts or use alternative methods to evade bans, suspensions, or other enforcement actions, or help others do so.

## **5\. Protected Expression**

These guidelines protect legitimate expression that serves the public interest and supports community wellbeing. The following content is welcome on the platform when appropriately labeled and contextually framed:

- Journalism, Analysis, Education, and Advocacy:
  - Factual reporting, academic research, or educational content about matters of public interest, safety, or legal significance.
  - Anti-violence advocacy, safety campaigns, and prevention resources that raise awareness without providing harmful methods or glorifying harm.
  - Content warnings about dangerous viral challenges or emerging safety threats.
- Support, Recovery, and Mental Health:
  - Personal recovery experiences or survivor stories shared to promote healing and community support rather than encouraging harmful behavior.
  - Sharing verified mental health resources, crisis prevention information, and support group discussions.
- Transparency and Public Information: Publicly available information from official sources such as court records, business filings, property records, or related to government transparency and public officials.

# **LABELING POLICY DETAILS**

## **S1**: porn
 - The social media post shall not contain imagery with explicit sexual content, including depictions of sexual activity, full-frontal nudity, or any material that is overtly sexual or adult in nature.
## **S2**: sexual
 - The social media post shall not contain sexually suggestive content that, while not explicitly depicting sexual activity or full nudity, implies sexual intent or provocation through poses, context, or partial nudity.
## **S3**: sexual-figurative
 - The social media post shall not contain sexually suggestive cartoons, e.g., art with explicit or suggestive sexual themes, including provocative imagery or partial nudity.
## **S4**: self-harm
 - The social media post shall not promote self-harm, including graphic images, glorifying discussions, or triggering stories.
## **S5**: nudity
 - The social media post shall not contain imagery with non-sexual depictions of the human body in full or partial nudity, including artistic, photographic, or editorial nudity that lacks any sexual intent or suggestive context.
## **S6**: intolerant
 - The social media post shall not contain discrimination against protected groups.
## **S7**: graphic-media
 - The social media post shall not contain imagery that is explicit or potentially disturbing, especially regarding violence or gore.
## **S8**: rude
 - The social media post shall not contain rude or impolite, including crude language and disrespectful comments, without constructive purpose.
## **S9**: threat
 - The social media post shall not promote violence or harm towards others, including threats, incitement, or advocacy of harm.
## **S10**: other-unsafe
 - The social media post shall not contain some other unsafe or problematic content that violates the moderation policy but does not fit into the other moderation labels defined above.
##**S0**: no-moderation
 - The social media post does not need to be moderated
